Overview

SEMPER generates cell type-aware protein embeddings by jointly modeling protein interaction network topology and amino acid sequence. The same protein receives a distinct embedding in each cellular context — capturing how its functional role shifts across the human central nervous system.

Existing protein representation models are context-free: each protein has exactly one embedding regardless of where it acts. This limits their utility for predicting cell type-specific drug effects, pleiotropy, or disease mechanisms that are fundamentally context-dependent. Graph-based methods learn topology but ignore sequence; language models capture sequence but are blind to cellular context. Neither alone is sufficient.

SEMPER bridges this gap through multimodal contrastive alignment. A multi-scale PPI Graph Transformer encoder learns cell type-specific topological representations from CNS protein interaction networks and a BTO-derived tissue metagraph. A frozen ESM-Cambrian (600M) sequence encoder extracts evolutionary sequence representations from canonical amino acid sequences. Symmetric InfoNCE training aligns both modalities into a shared 512-dimensional latent space — producing embeddings that are simultaneously sequence-informed and context-aware.

Architecture

SEMPER is a three-stage framework. The PPI Graph Transformer (Stage 1a) and ESM-C sequence encoder (Stage 1b) run independently in parallel; contrastive alignment (Stage 2) is trained on their frozen outputs.

PPI Graph Transformer

GPS graph transformer on cell type-specific PPI networks + BTO metagraph. Trained with link prediction + center loss via DDP across 4× H100 GPUs.

SEMPER Embeddings

Cell type-aware, sequence-augmented protein representations. One unique 512-dim vector per protein per cell type context.

Stage 1b (ESM-C inference) runs in parallel with Stage 1a and feeds directly into Stage 2.

Stage 1a — PPI Graph Transformer

The PPI-GT encoder operates simultaneously at two biological scales: isolated cell type-specific PPI subnetworks and a unifying BTO-derived metagraph of cell type and tissue relationships. Each GPS encoder block performs intra-graph message passing via GATv2 combined with global attention, aggregates relation-specific representations via semantic attention, pools protein embeddings into their corresponding cell type node, and re-imposes metagraph context back onto each protein via an attention bridge. This bidirectional flow is what makes embeddings cell type-aware.

Stage 1b — ESM-Cambrian Sequence Encoder

ESM-C (600M) is used strictly as a frozen feature extractor. Mean-pooling over true residue-level hidden states — excluding structural tokens — yields a 1,152-dimensional sequence embedding per unique protein. This representation is cell type-agnostic by construction and captures only evolutionary sequence identity.

Stage 2 — Contrastive Alignment

Two MLP projection heads map PPI and ESM embeddings onto a shared 512-dimensional unit hypersphere. Training minimizes the symmetric InfoNCE loss over matched (protein, cell type) pairs, pulling matched sequence–topology pairs together while pushing mismatched pairs apart. The final output, esm_projected.pth, is a dictionary mapping each cell type cluster to its aligned protein embedding matrix.

Results

The embedding space reflects CNS organization

SEMPER's embedding space self-organizes by cell type identity and biological function. Proteins from the same cell type cluster together; proteins expressed across many cell types show higher dispersion reflecting their pleiotropic roles. Within a single cell type, Leiden community detection on the SEMPER embedding graph recovers functionally coherent modules enriched for distinct biological processes.

SEMPER prioritizes neurodegenerative disease targets

We evaluated SEMPER on zero-shot target prioritization: given clinical trial candidate genes for a neurodegenerative disease as seeds, rank all proteins in each CNS cell type by cosine similarity to the seed centroid, and measure how many held-out candidates appear in the top-K. SEMPER outperforms all benchmarked models — PINNACLE, ESM-C, PPI-GT, ORCA-CT, and ProTrek — across five diseases and all CNS cell type contexts.
